What should you notice?

  • Could you identify the mirror and what to do without instructions beyond the game page?
  • After a failed run, could you explain why the light went where it did?
  • Did real-time movement add anticipation—or mostly pressure to react quickly?
  • Did manual stepping make decisions clearer, or make the route feel slow?
  • Were the controls comfortable for your input method (keyboard, mouse, touch or assistive technology)?
  • Would you choose to try a harder puzzle in either style?
